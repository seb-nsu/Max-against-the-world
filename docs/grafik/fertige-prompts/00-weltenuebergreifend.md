# Fertige Prompts: Weltenübergreifende Gegenstände

Erzeugt von `tools/prompts_zusammensetzen.py` aus `../00-stil-und-werkzeuge.md` (dort ändern, nicht hier).

Je Sammelbild den Block komplett in Gemini kopieren und Max' Konzeptblatt als Stilvorlage
anhängen, keine Skizze. Umrechnen mit `tools/figur.py` (Endesga-Palette), Ausschneiden je Zelle.

## Reisegepäck (2×5)

```text
Item sheet in exactly the same style as the attached character sheet of the boy Max: same line weight, same flat cel shading, light from upper left. Grid exactly as given in the motif, items in reading order left to right and top to bottom, each item complete and centered in its own invisible cell at the same scale, generous empty space between items, slight front-side view, items floating freely on a solid flat magenta (#ff00ff) background filling the whole image, no tiles, boxes, frames, panels or cell borders, no checkerboard, no ground shadow, no text, letters or numbers on any item. Do not draw the boy Max. 2x5 grid: a closed tin of tuna with a blue fish drawing on a blank label; the same tin opened, lid bent up, pink tuna in brine inside; the same tin empty and shiny inside; a wooden cooking spoon with a crust of dried yellow cheese on its bowl; a red-and-white striped woolen sock; a full-size round clay jug of olive oil with a handle and a cork, oil up to the neck; the same jug less full, a drip running down its side; a single golden sandal with two small white wings at the heel; rectangular black sunglasses; a small brown notebook with a pencil tucked in the spine.
```
